"Nat-so Kanna-cted" 1

Location: ES Building — Elevator

Kanna: ……

Mitsuru: Ahhh! Hey, wait! Don’t close the doors yeeet! Lemme innn!

Phew, I made it! Thanks… Oh, if it isn’t Kanna-chan!

Kanna: Rushing to get on is dangerous. Please refrain from doing it again.

Mitsuru: Ahaha. That sounds just like an announcement you’d hear on the train~.

But you’re right, I shouldn’t do that. Sorry.

Kanna: Which floor are you going to, Tenma-san?

Mitsuru: Wait, you’re gonna push the button for me? Thanks a bunch! I’m going to the RhyLink office on the twelfth floor!

Kanna: There is no need to thank me. I gauged that it would take less effort for me to move my finger than for you to walk over here and press the button yourself.

Mitsuru: Ahaha, okay, weird! But it is nice of you, so I can’t say I’m not grateful!

You’re always so poker-faced, but you’ve actually got a pretty warm heart~. I bet your body’s super cold!

Kanna: …I have deduced that you are referring to the old saying “cold hands, warm heart”.

However, that is merely a superstition. It originated from an English proverb, you see. 1

Mitsuru: Really? I had nooo idea. You sure know a lot, Kanna-chan!

I wanna be that smart someday... Ao-chan-senpai asked me to get groceries the other day and I immediately—

Ahhhh! It happened again! I forgot something someone asked me to do!

Kanna: Were you asked to buy groceries again? If you really are this forgetful, it would be helpful to note these things down.

Mitsuru: Don’t worry! I’ve been writing our shopping lists on my hand since then.

Kanna: ? Your hand…?

Mitsuru: Yeah, coz my hand’s always right in front of me! Even if I forget to write it on a piece of paper, if it’s on my hand I’ll remember right away! Smart, right☆

Kanna: So you forget that you even made a note in the first place.

It appears that you remembered without having written it down today. In that case, would it not be wiser to go grocery shopping before you forget?

Mitsuru: Oh, that’s not what I remembered. I meant something else, not the groceries thing.

Kanna: I believe the cause of your forgetfulness is your tendency to get distracted by passing thoughts.

Mitsuru: Ehehe~

Ah— If we keep talking about this, I’m gonna forget the thing I had to tell you!

Kanna: Me…?

Mitsuru: Yup. Anzu nee-chan’s been looking for you coz she needs to talk to you about something.

She said to tell you to contact her if I ran into you.

So it’s a good thing I did.

Y’know, I found you without even trying, but Anzu nee-chan said she’s been searching high and low and she still couldn’t find you anywhere. Kinda weird!

Kanna: Of course. This is because I have been taking the necessary steps such that I do not run into her.

I analysed Anzu-san’s schedule and movement patterns, made predictions, and ensured that I was constantly moving in the opposite direction as her.

Mitsuru: Whaaat?! B-but why?! I don’t get ya at all!

Why’re you avoiding Anzu nee-chan?

Kanna: Avoiding?

Mitsuru: That’s what “constantly moving in the opposite direction as her” sounds like to me. Am I wrong?

Kanna: ……

…A few days ago, I ran into Anzu-san. She mentioned personalised outfits.

Mitsuru: Oooh! She’s making one for you too? She made a super cool one for me~♪

Kanna: Yes. She said she was making outfits that “bring out each and every idol’s characteristics and charm”, and asked me to inform her if I had any requests.

Mitsuru: Yeah, okay.

Kanna: However, I have not been able to come up with a suitable answer, so I have simply been coordinating my movements in such a way that I do not run into her.

Mitsuru: Huh? Why?

Kanna: Because, in theory, I should have an answer, but I do not. We would be stuck in a loop of her following up on the topic and me replying, “I have not thought of anything yet,” ad infinitum.

It would be an incredibly irritating… No, an incredibly fruitless endeavour.

Therefore, I decided that not meeting with her until I thought of something would be most productive.

Mitsuru: Huuuuh~? I don’t really get what you’re saying…

Kanna: Is that so?

Mitsuru: Yeah. Anzu nee-chan’s been looking all over for ya. I really think you should talk to her.

Because if you don’t, I’ll be so sad for her…!

Kanna: How very irritating—

Never mind. Is that how it works?

On a related note, may I ask how your personalised outfit was tailored, Tenma-san?

Mitsuru: Who, me?

Kanna: Yes. I have decided that collecting information about personalised outfits as a point of reference is essential.

proofing: ciel

  1. while it is true that “cold hands, warm heart” was first recorded in a book called collectanea by v.s. lean—a collection of english and foreign proverbs published in bristol, england in 1903—it is also widely believed that this saying is a word-for-word translation of the french saying "froides mains, chaudes amours" (meaning “cold hands, warm loves”), first documented in 1710.
  2. the title of this story is a reference to the pun kanna makes in his first idol story. i have a translation note about it that you can read there!
