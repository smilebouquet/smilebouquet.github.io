"Nat-so Kanna-cted" 2

Mitsuru: Lemme think… I wanted my outfit to scream “I’m Tenma Mitsuru, the superstar that makes Ra*bits proud!” 1

Kanna: An outfit befitting a superstar? That is incredibly abstract.

Mitsuru: Yup. But I was in your shoes, too—I just couldn't picture what that would look like…

So I asked a buncha different people about their own outfits.

And then I remembered the moment I wanted to be an idol! Y’know, that very first zing.

No matter how fast I am, I’ll never outrun a car, right?

But when you’re an idol, your song can travel the whole world in an instant! 2

I made sure Anzu got what I meant, that I wanted to brighten people’s lives like the sun, and that’s why I’ll keep being an idol!

And she took that and made the coolest outfit in the world, just for me!

Kanna: I see. Our situations are far too dissimilar, and I am afraid I cannot use you as a reference. I shall file this information away regardless.

Mitsuru: You can’t?

Kanna: I do not believe so. Unlike you, how to articulate what I must remains inexplicit to me.

Mitsuru: In-explicit? You’re saying you still dunno?

Ermmm…

Oh, I got it! I know how you can tell someone something without using any words! They just need to get what you mean, right?

Kanna: …I hope you realise I do not possess cryptaesthesia… That is, I cannot send messages via telepathy.

Mitsuru: Geez, I wasn’t gonna suggest anything that sci-fi-ish…

Kanna: I had anticipated you doing exactly that.

Mitsuru: Rude! Not even I would do something so weird! Hmph!

My idea was that Anzu nee-chan could follow you around all day and get to know you better!

That way, she can make an outfit based on what she thinks makes you stand out!

…How about it?

Kanna: ……

I see. With your method, I might be able to resolve this irritating issue with minimal effort.

You have been quite helpful, Tenma-san.

Mitsuru: You’re welcome~!

A few days later

Location: ES Building

Kanna: ...Huh? “Good work”…?

Ah, might you be referring to the job I just completed?

It is nothing praiseworthy, really. I expended the same amount of time and effort as I did during the recording job yesterday.

Therefore, I would hardly consider this “work”, let alone “good” work. 3

…That is not what you meant?

The act of seeing a job through to the end still deserves to be told “good work”?

Is that so. I suppose that is a decent way to say it. It is straightforward and brief.

Oh, this is how I truly feel, yes. To me, energy conservation is something to be commended.

I am relieved you understand what I am saying, though I am rather irritated.

What? I am talking about the fact that I have to add footnotes to everything I say. Communicating with people really is irritating...

…Oh. You are correct—I have not eaten lunch yet.

Well, you have not eaten lunch either, have you Anzu-san? You have been following me around since morning, after all.

What? You think my irritation could be due to a lack of energy?

…

Understood.

Oh, no. I personally do not see a connection between those two things whatsoever. I simply believe it would be the most efficient use of my limited free time to get lunch immediately.

Yes. Let us get going.

Location: Staff Dining Hall

—Anzu-san, over here.

I have found us a seat. Its location is close to the tray return area, so I have deemed it as a good seat.

What? Yes, these chocolates are my lunch.

No. Quite the opposite, actually. Since it is past lunchtime, eating a large meal at this hour would not be ideal. There is still dinner to consider, after all.

It would disrupt my ideal daily routine.

Besides, I like chocolate.

My favourite food…? Let me think. Chocolate is an efficient way of replenishing energy, so I suppose it is a food that I favour.

? Why are you laughing? Is that really so strange?

That is true. I have come to terms with the fact that my principles do not align with the rest of society.

Oh. I may be eating only chocolate, but please do not let that stop you from enjoying your own meal.

Let us begin eating.

Were you able to collect enough information about myself after following me around for a day?

If I may be honest, my schedule today is no different from the usual, so I was beginning to wonder if this had been all for naught.

“It’s perfectly all right”…?

Is that so. I do not understand why you are enjoying yourself this much, but I am relieved to hear that you managed to do what you had set out to do.

What? I got chocolate on my cheek?

Is it over here?

…Umm, the other cheek, you say?

This is hopeless. The way human bodies are designed makes it so that I cannot see the area around my mouth.

Huh? I'm like a little kid?

Oh, but of course. I am just a little kid overflowing with hopes and dreams.

My lack of expression is nothing new, so there is no point in worrying about it.

What was that? You'll make an outfit that can make me smile?

I would advise against it. Such an endeavour has an 80 to 90% probability of failure.

…That is true. You will not know unless you try. In this world, there is no such thing as a success rate of 0%—only percentages that can be rounded down to zero.

I understand your enthusiasm, Anzu-san.

…I myself look forward to seeing what you make, if only a little.

proofing: ciel

  1. a reference to mitsuru’s first feature scout story, future superstar.
  2. this is something mitsuru originally says in tanabata festival. naturally, he also says it in future superstar.
  3. here, anzu originally says がんばってた (ganbatteta), which is the past continuous form of がんばる (ganbaru). ganbaru is commonly translated as “to do one’s best”, but it’s a lot closer in meaning to “to persevere and see something to the very end despite hardship”. it is apparently an important part of japanese culture and there are various articles about it; here’s one i found on wikipedia.

    anyway, kanna’s confusion originally comes from the fact that he was told that he “persevered” when, in his opinion, he worked no harder than he did the day before.
