Natsu Kanna: Idol Story 1

Time: Second year since the establishment of ES

Location: Just outside the StarPro office

Kanna: You have saved me, Anzu-san.

As you can see, I am only a child, so it would have been impossible for me to get away from a crowd of that size.

Well, If I had deliberated for long enough, I have no doubt that I could have come up a number of ways to amend my situation—running away, shaking them off, calling for help and getting them arrested. However…

Exerting brainpower on them of all things would be very irritating.

Very, very irritating...

…Oh. No, I am not lost. 1

I see you, too, are the type to be quick to judge others.

No, that was not meant to be criticism. I was simply cataloguing you.

I find conversations to be very irritating, you see.

Which is why I sort people into categories to the best of my ability, and aim to hold conversations with them using stock phrases prepared based on said categories.

I want to complete all conversations using only stock phrases such as “you get it,” “maybe so,” or “that’s nice.”

Computers can do it. Computers operate on nothing but numbers and their own programming.

Why can’t humans do the same?

Oh, no. I was not expecting you to answer that. I was simply voicing a question I have out loud.

Please do not worry. I process and answer all of my own questions in my own time.

Correct. I have no such expectations for other people. That includes you.

I am leaving. You have done me a great service today.

…Hm. Yes? What is it?

You are being quite the nagger—

Never mind. Yes, fine, did you need something else from me?

Oh. No, I am affiliated with ES.

I was recently employed as an idol. My name is Natsu Kanna.

Yes. I do act like a stray cat that refuses to acclimate to humans, as you have pointed out. You could even say I am “nat-so kanna-cted” to society. Funny, right? 2

Should I be smiling if it is? Smiling is irritating, though.

My life could be easier if I learned to at least smile politely, but I really do find it irritating.

Yes? Oh, so you know who I am? Of course.

I have come to the conclusion that I am a rare specimen with an inexplicable tendency to make headlines in newspapers or front pages of magazines.

The people chasing me earlier were all magazine reporters. They have been following me for a little while.

You cannot go a day in the entertainment industry without meeting at least one other genius, so I would have preferred if people refrained from making my existence such a big deal.

Unfortunately, it appears that when humans observe unexpected behaviours, they feel surprised and amused.

I envy that sort of sensibility.

Ah, I was not being sarcastic; I genuinely envy it. You know, having to constantly add footnotes to what I say is irritating.

Hm. Wait, are you asking if I’m famous…?

When you said you knew who I was, you meant as one of the new idols? You remembered my name and face from the roster?

I see. Yes, I understand the sort of person you are now.

Additionally, I believe I may have overrated myself just then.

I suppose there will always be people who don’t know who I am. Ha.

Ah, that was my first smile in fifteen days. My zygomatic muscles will be sore tomorrow.

Thank you. You have given me a rare learning experience.

…Hm? Yes, what is it now?

I am an idol at ES. It should not be difficult to understand that me walking around the area isn’t particularly peculiar.

Do you not understand that? If so, it would be irritating.

Hm… You think there could still be reporters lingering outside the building, and you want to call security just in case?

……

“A kind and gentle person”. “An outstanding member of society.” “A sacred goddess given human form”.

Out of these three options, which title pleases you most?

I was thinking of bestowing one upon you. I am grateful for your compassion, and believe your words deserve high praise.

However, I lack experience communicating with people, so I am unsure of which words are most impactful.

Hence, I deemed it fitting to allow you yourself to choose the title I am giving you.

That is all. …Have I done something wrong?

  1. one of kanna’s speech quirks (in this story, at least!) is his overuse of aizuchi. aizuchi are interjections used to show that the listener is paying attention to the speaker, and are particularly important in japanese culture.

    the aizuchi he uses most here is はい (hai, literally “yes”); for the sake of flow, i occasionally switch up whether i translate this into “oh” or “yes”.
  2. kanna is making a pun on his full name 名都神無 (read "natsu kanna") and the phrase 懐かんな (natsukan’na). 懐かん is an overly casual way of saying 懐かない (natsukanai), which is a word that is used to describe a person or animal who finds it difficult to warm up or open up to others.
