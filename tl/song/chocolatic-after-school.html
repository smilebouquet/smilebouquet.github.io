  1. 隠恋慕 (kakurenbou) is a pun that combines the japanese word for hide-and-seek, 隠れんぼ (kakurenbo), with 恋慕 (renbou) meaning “falling in love”.
  2. the original line goes ドキドキが1秒を追い越してく (dokidoki ga ichibyou wo oikoshiteku), which literally translates to “my heartbeats are surpassing one second”. If you think this sounds confusing... that's because it kind of is! at a glance, anyway.

    what this line is trying to convey is that the singer’s BPM—beats per minute—is typically 60 BPM (which is technically within the range of an average adult’s BPM of 60-100), but has increased due to nervousness as implied by the previous line. hence, each heartbeat becomes faster than one second and thus ‘surpasses’ it.
  3. a reference to the name of sora’s first !! event (投影★心を映す幻灯機, lit. “projection★the magic lantern that reflects the heart”).
  4. rare instance of me choosing to colour-code this by meaning instead of singing order (｡•ᴗ•｡)
