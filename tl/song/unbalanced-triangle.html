  1. the first-person pronoun used here is 俺 (ore), which is more masculine and 'rough' than 僕 (boku), and can imply a sort of power dynamic when used with people who aren't close with the speaker. this is, to my knowledge, the first time a knights song has ever used "ore" (trickstar has used it diamond summer, associated with the ! era main story summer live).
  2. 奪い合う (ubaiau) in a literal sense means "to fight over" (think people at target fighting over stanley cups when they were trending), but given the context of the song i decided to translate this to "romantic rival".
  3. lit. "you can't go back anymore".
