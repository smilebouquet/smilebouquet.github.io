  1. in music, a mashup is a song that is created by blending two or more pre-recorded songs together. technically speaking, however, the progression of splashing whirlpool is more reminiscent of a medley, which is where parts of existing pieces play one after another and occasionally can overlap. to illustrate the difference, here is an example of a mashup and a medley respectively.

    we may never get confirmation from the lyricist on why he chose one word over the other, but my speculation is that 'mashup', as a word that entered the english language more recently than 'medley', has an etymology that is rooted in modern english and is easier to grasp, thus making it easier to make the connection to the song's main motifs ("mixing together").
  2. in the official mv, yuuta is associated with sharks, specifically the great white shark. great white sharks have electroreceptors that allow them to detect things like temperature changes and muscle contractions in prey.
  3. 揚足を取る (ageashi wo toru) is a sumo move where you try to trip an opponent while they're trying to execute a move. it can also mean to use your opponent’s words against them. anyway, the mv does shows hinata trying to physically trip yuuta.
  4. a high-pressure area, or anticyclone, is an area near the surface of a planet where the atmospheric pressure is greater than the pressure in surrounding areas. here is the wikipedia article if you want to read about it more.
  5. the original line uses 最強 (saikyou, “strongest”), which is a reference to the phrase 最強の個人 (saikyou no kojin, “the strongest individual”). first mentioned in the ! era story repayment festival, this phrase is closely linked to 2wink’s development and has been mentioned in other stories such as night club.
